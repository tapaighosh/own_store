# Own Store: Vendor Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Own Store! This guide will walk you through setting up your store, adding products, and managing your inventory. We've designed the platform to be as simple as possible so you can focus on selling.

---

## Setting up your store for the first time

When you first log into your admin dashboard, you'll want to configure your store's appearance and basic details. 

1. **Go to the Settings Page**: Click on the **Settings** tab in the sidebar menu.
2. **Basic Information**: Enter your store's name. This will appear at the top of your website.
3. **Upload a Logo**: If you have a logo, upload it here. If not, your store name will be displayed in a clean, professional font.
4. **Choose your Theme**: 
   - Select a **Primary Color** (this controls the background and subtle accents).
   - Select an **Accent Color** (this controls buttons and important links).
   - Choose a **Font** that matches your brand's personality.
5. **Set up the Homepage Hero**: Write a catchy Headline and Subheadline that customers will see as soon as they land on your site. You can also upload a background image.
6. **Save**: Click **Save Settings** at the bottom of the page. Your live store will update instantly!

---

## Adding and managing products

Now that your store looks great, it's time to add what you're selling.

### Creating Categories
Before adding products, it helps to create categories (e.g., "T-Shirts", "Accessories").
1. Go to the **Categories** tab.
2. Click **Create Category**.
3. Enter a name and an optional description, then save.

### Adding your first product
1. Go to the **Products** tab and click **Add Product**.
2. **Details**: Enter the product name, price, and description. 
3. **Category**: Select one of the categories you just created.
4. **Images**: You can upload up to 4 images. The first image you upload will be the main image shown on the storefront.
5. **Inventory**: 
   - Set your current **Stock** level.
   - Set a **Low Stock Threshold**. (For example, if you set this to 5, the system will warn you when you only have 5 left).
6. **Status**: By default, new products are saved as **Draft**. When you're ready for customers to see it, change the status to **Active**.
7. **Featured**: Check the "Featured" box if you want this product to show up directly on your homepage.
8. Click **Save Product**.

---

## Updating your store information

Keeping your customers informed is important. You can update your contact information and social links at any time.

1. Go back to the **Settings** tab.
2. Scroll down to the **Footer & Contact** section.
3. Enter your support email, phone number, and physical address (if applicable).
4. Add your Instagram, Facebook, and Twitter links. 
5. Scroll down to the **SEO** section. Here you can add a short "Meta Title" and "Meta Description" — these are the words that show up on Google when people search for your store.
6. Click **Save Settings**.

You are now ready to sell!
